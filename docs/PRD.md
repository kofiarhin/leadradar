# LeadRadar V1 Product Requirements Document

**Status:** Approved requirements
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-08-28
**Repository:** `kofiarhin/leadradar`
**Implementation status:** Not started

## 1. Product Summary

LeadRadar is a reusable B2B prospecting application that turns public LinkedIn post engagement into a focused list of potential prospects who are strong candidates to contact and move toward booking a call.

The V1 product starts with one editable vertical profile for AI automation/software agencies while keeping the underlying workflow reusable for additional verticals later.

LeadRadar is not intended to be a general-purpose CRM, a LinkedIn clone, a mass lead database, an email infrastructure product, or an autonomous AI salesperson. It orchestrates external providers around a focused workflow and owns the durable prospect, signal, conversation, and opportunity intelligence generated by that workflow.

## 2. Core Outcome

> Identify potential prospects from LinkedIn post engagement who are strong candidates to contact and move toward booking a call.

The product should optimize for qualified commercial conversations and call-ready opportunities rather than raw scrape volume, email volume, or generic contact counts.

## 3. Product Promise

A user should be able to:

1. paste a publicly accessible LinkedIn post URL;
2. let LeadRadar extract public commenters;
3. identify which commenters match the configured ideal customer profile and show commercially relevant intent;
4. enrich qualified prospects with a verified business email where possible;
5. review and approve an AI-generated outbound sequence;
6. let the approved sequence run through the configured outreach provider;
7. have any genuine reply pause automated follow-ups immediately;
8. have incoming replies classified and prioritized;
9. see high-value prospects in an Opportunity Inbox; and
10. move strong conversations toward a booked call.

## 4. Target User

### 4.1 V1 user

LeadRadar V1 is a single-owner application intended for a B2B service operator who wants a repeatable way to turn relevant LinkedIn conversations into potential prospects.

The first validated vertical is:

- AI automation agencies;
- software agencies selling implementation/services;
- buyers such as Founders, CEOs, CTOs, COOs, Heads of Operations, and similar decision-makers.

### 4.2 Future users

The architecture should later support multiple workspaces, users, teams, saved vertical profiles, and other B2B industries without requiring a new lead-generation engine per niche.

Those capabilities are explicitly deferred from V1.

## 5. Problem Statement

Traditional outbound lead generation often begins with broad contact databases and weak intent signals. This creates several problems:

- large volumes of low-relevance leads;
- expensive enrichment on contacts that are unlikely to convert;
- generic outreach with little contextual relevance;
- manual review of replies to find real opportunities;
- duplicate contacts across campaigns;
- no durable relationship between the original public signal and eventual sales outcome.

LinkedIn discussions contain useful public commercial signals, particularly comments that express a problem, request, recommendation, evaluation, urgency, or active project. LeadRadar should turn those signals into a structured prospecting workflow.

## 6. Product Principles

1. **Signal before volume.** A smaller set of relevant commenters is preferable to a large scraped list.
2. **Qualify before enrichment.** Paid/limited enrichment should only be consumed for prospects worth contacting.
3. **Human approval before outbound.** No campaign may begin sending before the user reviews and approves its sequence and qualified batch.
4. **Deterministic safety rules.** Suppression, reply pausing, email verification gates, authentication, and policy enforcement must not depend on an AI judgment.
5. **AI for judgment-heavy work.** NVIDIA AI is used for prospect qualification, reply classification, and drafting.
6. **Provider independence.** External capabilities are replaceable adapters, not the LeadRadar source of truth.
7. **Durable prospect intelligence.** Qualified prospects and their signals/outcomes should accumulate across campaigns.
8. **Simple V1.** Avoid building CRM, mailbox, calendar, workflow-builder, or microservice complexity before the core loop is proven.

## 8. Locked V1 Service Boundaries

| Capability | V1 provider | LeadRadar responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn public-comment extraction | Apify | initiate discovery, normalize results, persist signals |
| AI qualification / reply intelligence / drafting | NVIDIA API | prompt context, schemas, validation, business workflow |
| Business-email discovery | Hunter | decide who may be enriched, persist provenance/results |
| Email verification | Hunter | enforce verification gate before outreach |
| Email sequences and reply integration | Hunter | campaign approval, lead state, conversation/opportunity state |
| Application database | MongoDB Atlas | authoritative LeadRadar data store |
| Hosting / webhook endpoint / worker | Heroku | application runtime and worker scaling |

Provider capability and exact endpoints must be revalidated before implementation. A provider limitation must not silently weaken locked product behavior; the adapter must fail clearly or be replaced by a compatible provider.

## 9. Primary User Journey

### 9.1 Configure the vertical

The owner edits the V1 vertical profile with:

- offer description;
- target job roles/seniority;
- target industries;
- preferred company-size range;
- target/allowed regions where useful;
- positive intent/buying signals;
- negative/exclusion signals;
- outreach goal (`BOOK_CALL` for V1);
- preferred outreach tone.

### 9.2 Create campaign

The owner enters:

- campaign name;
- public LinkedIn post URL.

LeadRadar validates that the URL is syntactically supported and creates a campaign immediately. Discovery then continues asynchronously.

### 9.3 Discover commenters

LeadRadar starts the Apify engagement adapter.

Only public LinkedIn comments are in V1 scope.

For each usable comment, LeadRadar captures at minimum when available:

- source post URL;
- public profile URL or provider-stable identity;
- display name;
- comment text;
- comment/provider identifier;
- discovery timestamp;
- optional role/company metadata returned by the provider.

### 9.4 Normalize and deduplicate

LeadRadar resolves each comment to a canonical prospect where possible.

The same person discovered in multiple campaigns should update the existing prospect and add a new Signal record instead of creating a duplicate prospect.

### 9.5 Qualify

NVIDIA receives:

- vertical profile;
- prospect identity metadata;
- relevant public engagement signal(s).

It returns a schema-valid result containing:

- `decision`: `QUALIFIED | REVIEW | REJECTED`;
- confidence;
- concise reason.

Only `QUALIFIED` continues automatically to enrichment.

`REVIEW` requires manual resolution.

`REJECTED` stops automatic processing for that campaign.

### 9.6 Enrich and verify

For qualified prospects, LeadRadar asks the Hunter enrichment adapter to find a business email and verify it.

Contact results must distinguish at least:

- `VERIFIED`;
- `NOT_FOUND`;
- `INVALID`;
- `REVIEW`/uncertain.

Only an acceptable verified business email may continue automatically.

Personal/free-mail addresses are not eligible for automatic B2B outreach in V1 and should be routed to review or skipped.

### 9.7 Apply outreach policy

LeadRadar evaluates a deterministic region-aware outreach policy before a lead is eligible to send.

Policy output:

- `ALLOWED` — the configured policy permits automated outreach;
- `REVIEW` — the user must make a manual decision;
- `BLOCKED` — automated outreach is prohibited by LeadRadar policy.

Unknown or insufficient jurisdiction/business-type information defaults to `REVIEW`, not `ALLOWED`.

The V1 policy system is a product safety/control layer, not legal advice. Production expansion into jurisdictions must be reviewed against applicable requirements.

### 9.8 Suppression check

Immediately before sending, LeadRadar checks the global workspace suppression store and active relationship state.

Automatic outreach must be blocked for records including:

- unsubscribe/do-not-contact;
- complaint;
- hard bounce;
- duplicate active outreach;
- existing active conversation where another automated sequence would be inappropriate.

### 9.9 Generate campaign copy

NVIDIA creates a short 2–3 email sequence using:

- vertical profile;
- campaign/source-post context;
- offer;
- outreach goal;
- approved tone.

The generated sequence is a draft only.

The user must be able to edit the copy.

### 9.10 Campaign approval

Before any first outbound message, LeadRadar shows the qualified/verified/eligible batch and final sequence copy.

The owner explicitly approves the campaign.

Only after that approval may leads be added to the Hunter sequence automatically.

Any material change to approved sending copy or eligibility rules should require re-approval before additional unsent prospects are released.

### 9.11 Send and monitor

Hunter owns mailbox connection and delivery infrastructure.

LeadRadar owns:

- which prospect was released to outreach;
- provider references;
- current outreach state;
- send/reply events needed by the product;
- campaign/opportunity relationships.

### 9.12 Process reply

When a genuine prospect reply is received:

1. deduplicate the provider event;
2. persist the inbound message;
3. pause that prospect's automated sequence immediately;
4. update the outreach state;
5. then run NVIDIA reply classification.

AI must never be required to decide whether a genuine reply should pause automated follow-ups.

### 9.13 Classify and create opportunity

Reply classifications for V1:

- `POSITIVE`;
- `QUESTION`;
- `LATER`;
- `REFERRAL`;
- `NEGATIVE`;
- `UNSUBSCRIBE`;
- `OUT_OF_OFFICE`;
- `REVIEW` for uncertain classification.

Relevant commercial responses create or update an Opportunity.

A positive response can be elevated to `READY_TO_BOOK` when the conversation indicates a call is an appropriate next action.

### 9.14 Draft response

NVIDIA may draft the next reply using the conversation history, prospect context, vertical profile, and latest intent classification.

The user must review/edit and explicitly send the reply.

No autonomous conversational sending is permitted in V1.

### 9.15 Book call

LeadRadar does not own scheduling in V1.

The user shares their existing calendar link manually or arranges the meeting externally.

The user marks the opportunity as `BOOKED` when confirmed.

## 11. Domain Definitions

### Prospect

A canonical person LeadRadar has discovered. A Prospect is not automatically qualified or contactable.

### Signal

A public engagement event connected to a Prospect, Campaign, and source post. V1 signals are LinkedIn comments.

### Qualified Prospect

A Prospect whose identity and signal context meet the configured vertical requirements strongly enough to proceed to enrichment.

### Verified Contact

A qualified Prospect with an acceptable verified business email.

### Conversation

A durable message thread associated with a Prospect and outreach relationship.

### Opportunity

A Prospect whose conversation indicates a meaningful commercial next step.

### Suppression

A durable rule preventing automated contact for an address/prospect within the workspace.

## 12. Data Retention Requirements

### Qualified prospects

Qualified prospects are durable LeadRadar records and may accumulate signals/outcomes across campaigns.

### Rejected discovery records

Rejected discovery data should be retained for 30 days by default for deduplication, audit, and pipeline debugging, then deleted or minimized according to the final implementation's retention process.

### Review records

Retain until resolved or until an explicit retention process applies.

### Suppression

Retain the minimum durable suppression information necessary to prevent future automated contact, even when broader prospect data is removed.

### Raw provider payloads

Do not retain indefinitely by default. Persist normalized product data and only the provider metadata necessary for traceability, retries, and debugging.

## 13. Authentication Requirements

V1 uses a single-owner account.

- admin identity is seeded/configured from server-side environment configuration;
- no public signup;
- no OAuth required;
- no team management;
- password must not be stored in plaintext;
- authenticated sessions use secure HTTP-only cookies;
- session data must be server-verifiable;
- all business records remain scoped by `workspaceId` to preserve a path to multi-tenant SaaS.

## 14. Global Outreach Policy Requirements

LeadRadar discovery, qualification, and storage are global.

Automatic outreach is controlled separately by a deterministic policy layer.

Requirements:

- policy rules are versionable/configurable;
- the decision and policy version used are persisted for an outreach eligibility decision;
- unknown jurisdictions or ambiguous prospect/business types default to `REVIEW`;
- suppression always overrides an `ALLOWED` policy decision;
- AI must not override a deterministic `BLOCKED` policy decision;
- policy changes must apply before future sends;
- implementation must support future country/region policy expansion without rewriting prospecting logic.

## 15. AI Requirements

NVIDIA is the V1 runtime intelligence provider.

AI is restricted to three core capabilities:

### 15.1 Prospect qualification

Must return validated structured output.

### 15.2 Reply classification

Must return validated structured output.

### 15.3 Draft generation

Produces editable copy only; user remains the sender for replies and campaign approval remains required for sequences.

### 15.4 AI failure behavior

- schema-invalid output must not advance the workflow;
- low-confidence/uncertain results route to `REVIEW`;
- bounded retry may be applied for transient/format failures;
- no AI failure may bypass suppression, verification, approval, authentication, or policy gates.

## 16. Non-Functional Requirements

### 16.1 Scalability

- Express request handlers must not wait for the whole pipeline;
- long-running work is handled asynchronously;
- web and worker processes scale independently;
- provider concurrency can be bounded independently;
- unbounded histories such as Signals and Messages are stored separately from the Prospect snapshot;
- provider implementations remain replaceable behind interfaces.

### 16.2 Reliability

- webhook processing is idempotent;
- jobs are retryable with bounded attempts/backoff;
- duplicate provider delivery must not duplicate messages, signals, enrichment spend, or outreach;
- partial pipeline failure is visible and recoverable;
- no automatic retry of a state-changing send operation unless idempotency is guaranteed.

### 16.3 Security

- provider/API credentials are server-only;
- secrets use Heroku configuration/environment variables;
- secrets never appear in client bundles or logs;
- webhook authenticity must be validated where provider capability permits;
- workspace authorization is enforced server-side;
- sensitive prospect/conversation data is not included unnecessarily in logs;
- login and state-changing endpoints require CSRF-aware/session-safe design appropriate to the chosen cookie strategy.

### 16.4 Observability

Every asynchronous operation should be traceable by relevant identifiers such as:

- `workspaceId`;
- `campaignId`;
- `prospectId`;
- `jobId`;
- provider;
- operation/event type.

## 17. Success Metrics

### 17.1 North-star outcome

Potential prospects progressed to meaningful commercial conversations and call-ready opportunities.

### 17.2 Primary product metrics

- qualified prospects per source post;
- verified-contact rate among qualified prospects;
- reply rate among contacted prospects;
- positive/opportunity rate among replies;
- ready-to-book opportunities per campaign;
- booked calls per campaign/source post;
- cost per qualified prospect;
- cost per positive opportunity.

### 17.3 Metrics that must not become the primary success measure

- total profiles scraped;
- total emails found;
- total emails sent;
- email opens alone.

## 18. V1 Acceptance Criteria

LeadRadar V1 is functionally acceptable when all of the following are demonstrated:

1. the owner can authenticate and unauthenticated access to protected application data is blocked;
2. the owner can edit the single V1 vertical profile;
3. the owner can create a campaign from a syntactically valid public LinkedIn post URL;
4. campaign creation returns without waiting for the complete discovery pipeline;
5. public comment results can be ingested without storing LinkedIn credentials/session cookies;
6. duplicate public comments/provider events do not create duplicate Signals;
7. the same public profile discovered across campaigns resolves to one canonical Prospect where identity is sufficient;
8. NVIDIA qualification output is schema validated;
9. only `QUALIFIED` prospects advance automatically to enrichment;
10. `REVIEW` prospects require manual resolution;
11. rejected prospects do not consume automatic outreach resources;
12. Hunter enrichment distinguishes verified, not found, invalid, and uncertain outcomes;
13. only acceptable verified business emails may continue automatically;
14. region-aware policy returns `ALLOWED`, `REVIEW`, or `BLOCKED` and unknown cases do not default to allowed;
15. suppression blocks outreach regardless of qualification or policy allowance;
16. NVIDIA can create a 2–3 email sequence draft;
17. the owner can edit the draft;
18. no outbound campaign begins before explicit campaign approval;
19. an approved eligible batch can be released to Hunter without duplicate lead creation;
20. any genuine reply pauses future automated follow-ups for that prospect before AI classification;
21. incoming provider events/messages are idempotently ingested;
22. NVIDIA reply classification is schema validated;
23. positive/question/later/referral replies can create or update an Opportunity;
24. unsubscribe creates durable suppression and prevents future automated contact;
25. Opportunity Inbox prioritizes commercially relevant replies;
26. NVIDIA can generate an editable response draft;
27. the user remains responsible for sending conversational replies in V1;
28. the user can mark an Opportunity `READY_TO_BOOK` and `BOOKED` manually;
29. qualified prospects remain searchable across campaigns;
30. rejected discovery data follows the configured 30-day retention policy;
31. provider failures are visible and do not silently skip safety/eligibility gates;
32. additional worker capacity can process independent jobs without corrupting prospect/campaign state.

## 19. Initial V1 Release Boundary

The first release should prove this vertical slice before expansion:

```text
Public LinkedIn post URL
→ public commenters
→ canonical prospects/signals
→ NVIDIA qualification
→ verified business email
→ outreach policy + suppression
→ reviewed campaign sequence
→ approved Hunter outreach
→ reply ingestion and immediate pause
→ NVIDIA intent classification
→ Opportunity Inbox
→ human-led move toward a booked call
```

Additional verticals, CRM depth, calendar integrations, mailbox connections, and broader channel automation are expansion work only after this loop is verified with real usage.
